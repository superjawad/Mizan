التراث — Heritage

The craft
behind the
salon

The Moroccan salon is not furniture — it is architecture. Built into the walls of a room, the seddari defines the social geometry of the home. Mizan encodes this knowledge so that what you design can actually be built.

الصندوق SandoqWooden base and storage coffre. 30 cm high, jointed up to 2 m.
المخدى MkhadyBack cushions, ~60 cm wide, tiled across the full span.
المزاود MzaoudCylindrical bolsters marking the end of each bench segment.
الميدة MidaCentral pedestal table with 40 cm legroom clearance.
